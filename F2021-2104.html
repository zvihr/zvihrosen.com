Description

Discrete Mathematics is the field of mathematics studying discrete structures such as finite sets, graphs, and networks. These structures are particularly important in computer science. Because the objects are relatively easy to define, they also provide an excellent opportunity to develop mathematical proof-writing and logical reasoning.

From the FAU Catalog: A proof-oriented approach to and applications of propositional logic, sets, functions, relations, combinatorics, graphs, and trees.
