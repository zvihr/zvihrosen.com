Description

The third course in the calculus sequence, covering vectors and the geometry of space, vector-valued functions, functions of several variables, partial derivatives and gradients, optimization with Lagrange multipliers, multiple integrals in rectangular, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates, and vector calculus including line and surface integrals, Green's, Stokes', and the Divergence Theorems.

All class business including grades and lecture notes will be managed on the Canvas site.
